Alison Hayes has a long history with disability and disabling conditions. She managed a mild learning disability in childhood, confusing her classmates by being in both advanced classes and the “resource room”. She was diagnosed with depression at the age of 9, and had her first unexplainable symptoms within a year or two of that. While in college, she started having strange movement symptoms, leading to an eventual diagnosis of conversion disorder(now Functional Neurological Disorder) in 2003. Since then, she:

-got on to SSDI
-got trained and certified in geographic information systems(GIS)
-discovered new muscles by pulling, straining or spraining them
-taught college courses in GIS
-Taught a lot of doctors about FND
-got a master’s degree in organizational change management
-regained the ability to safely use stairs, and
-Started her own business. Twice.

She currently runs [Thriving While Disabled](www.thrivingwhiledisabled.com), a coaching business and blog to encourage others with disabilities to take back control of their lives. She can be found on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/thrivingwhiledisabled/), [Twitter](@Thrivingwdisabl), or [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/thrivingwhiledisabled/)

Personal: Alison lives with her partner, Alvaro(Al), their cats, Nigel and Rorschach, and recently moved in with Al’s parents, Alvaro and Vivian. She’s proud to have a cat named for both an antihero comic book character and a psychiatrist!

I think a lot about the interplay between physical and mental health. I also think a lot about intersectional identities and the process of creating a new normal after diagnosis. I do have a history of depression and anxiety and put a fair amount of energy into managing my expectations and detailed pre-planning to help manage my life.

Topics: interplay between physical and mental health, anxiety, depression, FND, pre-planning, and self-advocacy
