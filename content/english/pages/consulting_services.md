Alison Hayes is an author and disability rights advocate with the lived experience to back it all up. She has been primarily supported by SSDI since 2004 and has a masters degree in organizational change management. She has used her natural curiosity and interest in how systems work to deeply understand every support program she has investigated and has delved into related topics with a focus on the rights of and supports for people with disabilities.
